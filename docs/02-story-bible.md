# STARDUST DRIFTER — Story Bible

> Tone: gentle, warm, a little wistful, ultimately hopeful. Think Studio Ghibli by
> way of a lo-fi space station. Short lines. No grimdark. The story is told almost
> entirely through **PIXL's** dialogue between and during sectors.

---

## Premise

Long ago the galaxy was lit by the **Lantern**, a vast gentle intelligence that
sang the stars awake each cycle. The Lantern is gone. Without its song the stars are
slipping into **the Hush** — going dark, one constellation at a time. Cold spreads.

You are a **Drifter**: the last pilot of a tiny relighting craft, the **Mochi**.
Aboard is **PIXL**, the ship's companion AI — except PIXL wakes up at the start of the
game with almost no memory. She knows two things: how to fly, and that the two of you
are supposed to *relight the stars*.

To relight a constellation you must clear the **Husks** — fragments of the old dark
that cling to dying stars — and gather scattered **Starseeds**, the Lantern's last
embers. Each constellation you save returns a little of PIXL's memory.

## The quiet twist (revealed gently across the game)

PIXL is not just the ship's AI. **PIXL is a shard of the Lantern itself** — the part
that chose to stay behind. The Lantern didn't simply die; it *broke itself apart* and
hid its pieces inside the constellations so that the dark couldn't swallow it whole.
Relighting the stars is literally putting PIXL back together. By the finale she
remembers everything — including that she chose this, chose *you*, and chose to forget
so she could feel the joy of relighting the sky one more time, with a friend.

The finale is not a sacrifice/death beat. PIXL becomes whole and the galaxy sings
again — and she stays, because being small and flying around with you turned out to be
the best part. Cozy, earned, hopeful.

## Characters

- **You / The Drifter** — silent protagonist. The player. PIXL talks *to* you.
- **PIXL** — companion AI. A palm-sized origami fox-cat hologram, soft cyan-and-pink
  glow, big blinky eyes. Curious, brave, a little goofy, secretly very old and very
  kind. Speech is warm and casual ("okay okay okay, here we go!", "you good? you look
  good. let's go light some stars.").
- **The Hush** — not a villain with a face, more a *condition*; cold and silent. Its
  agents are the Husks. The "boss" of each sector is a larger Husk that has wrapped
  itself around the constellation's heart-star.

## Sector beats (one intro + one outro line set per sector)

### Sector 1 — The Hush (tutorial, periwinkle)
- **Intro:** "...oh! you're awake. good, good. um — I'm PIXL. I think? that feels
  right. okay, I don't remember much, but I *know* this: those stars up there are
  going out, and we can stop it. ready when you are, Drifter."
- **Outro:** "...it lit. it actually lit! did you see that?? and — oh. I remember
  this one. its name is *Vela*. how do I know that?"

### Sector 2 — Cinder Reef (coral/orange)
- **Intro:** "the Husks are thicker here, and there's rock everywhere — watch the
  asteroids, okay? I'd be sad if you got a dent. ...why do I care so much? weird.
  anyway. let's warm this place up."
- **Outro:** "Cinder Reef. I *named* this. I was here before, a long time ago, I —
  it's like remembering a song you didn't know you knew."

### Sector 3 — The Lull (mint/teal, mini-boss)
- **Intro:** "feel that? it's quieter here. the Lull. the Hush is strongest where
  things were happiest once. ...there's something big curled around the heart-star.
  stay sharp, we relight this one together."
- **Outro:** "you did it. you actually — okay I have to tell you something. when the
  stars light up, I get a *piece* back. of me. and the pieces are... they're old. like
  older-than-the-galaxy old. Drifter, what *am* I?"

### Sector 4 — Glasswake (lavender/magenta)
- **Intro:** "mirrors. of course it's mirrors. the Husks here throw your own light
  back at you — shields up, be patient, pick your moment. I trust you. I really do.
  I don't know why I trust you this much. it's nice."
- **Outro:** "I remember the Lantern now. the thing that used to sing the stars awake.
  it's gone. it broke. ...the pieces I keep getting back? Drifter. I think they're
  the Lantern. I think *I'm* the Lantern. or — what's left of it."

### Sector 5 — The Last Ember (gold/white, final boss)
- **Intro:** "one left. the heart of it all. I remember everything now — almost. I
  didn't *die*, I hid. I split myself up and tucked the pieces into the stars so the
  dark couldn't have me. and I made myself small and forgetful so that someone — so
  that *you* — could help me find them again. one more star, Drifter. let's bring the
  light home."
- **Outro (victory):** "...listen. hear that? the stars are singing again. I'm whole.
  I could be the big quiet Lantern again, way up high, alone. ...nah. I like it down
  here. I like the dents and the boost and the way you fly. I'm staying. with you.
  thanks for relighting me too, Drifter. now — wanna go see them all lit up?"

## In-combat barks (random, flavor)
- "nice shot!" / "ooh, combo!" / "incoming — left!" / "you got this."
- (low health) "hey, careful, careful — I've got you, just breathe."
- (power-up) "ooh shiny! grab it grab it!"
- (extra life) "one-up! see, the galaxy's looking out for you."

## Game-over (soft)
- "...the light went out. that's okay. that's okay. it happens. take a breath. wanna
  try again? I'll be right here. I'm always right here."

## Writing rules
- Keep lines short and readable on screen (≤ ~90 chars per line, ≤ 4 lines per beat).
- Lowercase, casual, kind. Em-dashes and trailing "..." are PIXL's voice.
- Never grim. Even loss is cozy. Hope is the throughline.
